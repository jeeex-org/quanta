# Countries & Geography — Roadmap

## Overview

Comprehensive countries and geography domain organized by region: Asia, Europe, Africa, Americas, and Oceania. Covers geography, culture, politics, economics, and travel. All modules are currently planned — no geography implementations exist in `lib/std/` yet.
